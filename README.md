# Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

**Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** is a *2018 animated superhero film* produced by Columbia Pictures and Sony Pictures Animation. It follows teenager **Miles Morales** as he becomes the new Spider-Man and teams up with other Spider-People from different dimensions to save New York City.

## Why I Love This Movie
* **Stunning Animation:** The comic-book visual style looks unique in every single frame.
* **Great Soundtrack:** The music fits the energy and emotion of the story perfectly.
* **Relatable Story:** Miles' journey shows that anyone can wear the mask and be a hero.

## Top 3 Favorite Characters
1. Miles Morales
2. Peter B. Parker
3. Gwen Stacy

> "Anyone can wear the mask. You could wear the mask. If you didn't know that before, I hope you do now." — *Miles Morales*
